What's new in Acme Notes 2.4

  • Faster search. Full-text search is now about twice as fast on large workspaces.
  • Shared folders. Team plans can share whole folders, not just single notes.
  • Export. Export any note to Markdown from the note menu.
  • Fixes. Fixed a bug where pinned notes lost their order after sync.

As always, the update rolls out gradually over the next week.
