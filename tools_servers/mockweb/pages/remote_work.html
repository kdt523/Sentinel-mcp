7 tips for staying focused while working remotely

  1. Keep a fixed start time.
  2. Use a separate space for work.
  3. Batch your messages twice a day.
  4. Take a real lunch break away from the screen.
  5. Write tomorrow's top three tasks before you log off.
  6. Turn off non-essential notifications.
  7. Walk for ten minutes after every long call.
