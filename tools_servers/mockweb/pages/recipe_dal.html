Weeknight dal in 30 minutes

A comforting red lentil dal that needs one pot and pantry staples.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup red lentils
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • Salt, ghee, coriander

Method

  1. Rinse lentils and simmer with turmeric for 15 minutes.
  2. Fry cumin and onion in ghee, add tomatoes, cook down.
  3. Combine, season, and finish with coriander.
